SINCE 1992

Our Story

For over three decades, Annapurna Foods has been crafting authentic Indian delicacies that bring families together. Every product we create carries the warmth of tradition and the promise of purity.

THE BEGINNING

A Legacy of Flavour

It started in a small kitchen in the heart of India, where a family passionate about food decided to share their cherished recipes with the world. What began as a humble home enterprise quickly blossomed into Annapurna Foods — a name synonymous with authenticity, quality, and love.

Today, we serve thousands of families across India and beyond, offering a curated selection of sweets, namkeen, pickles, masalas, and dry fruits — all made with the same dedication that started it all. We believe that food is more than nourishment; it's a bridge between generations, cultures, and hearts.
